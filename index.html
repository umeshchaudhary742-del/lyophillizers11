03 / FAIR PLAY & OPPORTUNITY

TEAM RULES & OPPORTUNITIES

One team, equal respect, and opportunities built around interest and willingness to participate.

  1. Fair PlayPlay with honesty, sportsmanship, and respect.
  2. Interest-Based ParticipationPlaying opportunities are based on each player's interest, willingness, and availability.
  3. Equal OpportunitiesEvery interested player gets a fair chance to participate and contribute.
  4. Rational DecisionsMake reasonable decisions while considering everyone's interests.
  5. TransparencyCommunicate match participation, batting order, bowling opportunities, and responsibilities openly.
  6. No FavoritismTreat everyone fairly, without personal bias or special preferences.
  7. Commitment & DisciplineShare availability in advance and respect team decisions.
  8. Mutual RespectRespect teammates, their opinions, and individual interests.
  9. Voluntary ParticipationNo one should feel forced to play; participation should be willing and genuine.
  10. Team UnityPromote friendship, enjoyment, fairness, and cooperation.
